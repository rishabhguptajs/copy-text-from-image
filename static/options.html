How to use

  • Right-click any image → Copy text from image.
  • Press … and drag a box over anything: video frames, charts, text the page won't let you select.
  • The text lands on your clipboard. Paste it anywhere.

Privacy

Text recognition happens entirely on your computer. Screenshots and text never leave your browser. The only network request this extension ever makes is a one-time license key check. No analytics, no tracking, and this extension will never be sold.
